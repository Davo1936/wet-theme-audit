# From the WET Team

The website reads approved staff contributions from Shopify's `WET Staff Voice` metaobject definition. Each contribution is a separate entry, so new voices accumulate without replacing earlier ones.

## Add a contribution

1. In Shopify Admin, open **Content → Metaobjects → WET Staff Voice**.
2. Select **Add entry**.
3. Complete:
   - **Title** — a short editorial heading.
   - **Topic** — for example, `Atmosphere & respect`, `First visits`, `Events` or `Venue care`.
   - **Contribution** — the manager-approved staff wording.
   - **Staff name** — a first name, first name and role, or `WET venue team` when anonymous.
   - **Role** — optional.
   - **Publication date**.
   - **Feature on website** — enable this when the contribution should appear in homepage and First Visit highlights.
   - **Optional staff photo** — only with the staff member's approval.
4. Set the entry status to **Active** and save.

## Where entries appear

- Featured entries appear on the homepage.
- One featured entry appears on First Visit.
- Every active entry appears on `/pages/from-the-team`.
- Draft entries remain private.

## Editorial standard

- Management approves every contribution before activation.
- Preserve the staff member's meaning while correcting spelling and improving clarity.
- Do not publish complaints about identifiable patrons, operational disputes or personal information.
- Formal safety and conduct rules remain official WET policy, separate from individual staff perspectives.
